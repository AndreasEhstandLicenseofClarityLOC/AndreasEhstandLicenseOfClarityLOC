## About

Author of the AUGMANITAI term system — an independent, self-developed, DOI-deposited compendium that applies professional terminology-science conventions (ISO 704 / 1087 / 30042 as design goal) to the human–AI interface. The author is not aware of a prior, comparable compendium in this specific form.

**Academic background:** Former Distinguished Research Associate at TU Dortmund (IFS, Prof. Dr. Sabine Hornberg) and University of Bayreuth. Youngest holder of the Zertifikat Hochschullehre Bayern. Former ITF sport coach and Bundesliga trainer.
